## Reported sound and USB disconnects

The owner reports a short relay-like clank and beep roughly hourly, including
around 08:01 on 2026-11-13, without the room lights visibly going out. A brief
mains disturbance and transfer to battery is one possible explanation; lights
remaining on do not exclude a short disturbance. The
[Eaton 5P manual](https://www.eaton.com/content/dam/eaton/products/backup-power-ups-surge-it-power-distribution/backup-power-ups/eaton-5p-ups/user-guides/eaton-5p-ups-installation-and-user-manual.pdf)
describes silent AVR operation and a beep every 10 seconds on battery. The
sound alone cannot establish a transfer or its cause.

The Pi kernel journal showed **624 disconnects** on the 1550's `1-1.1` USB
path in the checked 12-hour window, with a median interval of 65.28 seconds;
one occurred at **08:01:13**. No disconnect was recorded for the 1150's
`1-1.2` path in that window. The 1550 USB device's `power/control` was `on`
when checked. The disconnect cadence is much shorter than the reported
hourly sound, and their relationship is unknown. USB disconnection is not
proof of a mains loss, battery transfer, or interrupted UPS output.

To investigate, inspect the 1550's front-panel **Fault log** around 08:01 and
check the other UPS too. Isolate the unstable USB connection by comparing
cables and hub ports, taking care to track which path follows which UPS. Set
up continuous, timestamped, read-only NUT monitoring for both units after
resolving the connection; sampled readings can still miss short events. Keep
UPS event and Pi clocks in mind when correlating records.
